Privacy Policy

Acru by Acru Studio — Last updated: April 4, 2026

Acru ("we," "our," or "the app") is a creative marketplace connecting film, TV, and commercial production professionals. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and password (stored securely via Firebase Authentication).

Profile information: Name, primary role, bio, experience level, genres, vibe tags, production types, location (city-level), profile photos, reel URLs, and credits. All provided voluntarily by you.

Usage data: We collect information about how you use the app, including interactions (sparks, pings, pitches, messages), projects posted, and feature usage. This helps us improve the experience.

Device and location data: With your permission, we collect approximate location data to show you relevant projects and collaborators in your area. We collect city-level location only, not precise GPS coordinates for tracking purposes.

Payment information: Subscriptions and purchases are processed through Apple's In-App Purchase system and RevenueCat. We do not store your credit card or payment details directly. Apple and RevenueCat handle payment processing under their own privacy policies.

Communication preferences: We collect your preference for receiving marketing communications (opt-in during onboarding, adjustable at any time in the app).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account and profile
  • To match you with relevant talent and projects based on your role, genres, vibes, and location
  • To enable messaging between matched users
  • To process in-app purchases and manage your subscription
  • To send you notifications about matches, pings, invites, and pitch responses
  • To enforce our community guidelines (block/report functionality)
  • To improve the app experience and fix bugs
  • To analyze usage patterns and trends in aggregate to improve our matching algorithm, features, and overall user experience. This data is anonymized and never used to identify individual users.
  • To send you occasional updates about new features, platform news, and community highlights, if you have opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time from the app settings or via the unsubscribe link in any email.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Other users: Your profile information (name, role, bio, photos, reel, credits, location) is visible to other Acru users as part of the marketplace experience.
  • Service providers: We use Firebase (Google) for authentication, database, and storage; RevenueCat for subscription management; Apple for payment processing. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies.
  • Email communications: If you opt in to marketing emails, your email address may be shared with our email service provider solely for the purpose of delivering those communications. You can opt out at any time.
  • Analytics: We may use anonymized, aggregated data to analyze trends and improve the app. This data cannot be used to identify individual users.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect the safety of our users.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Firebase (Google Cloud) servers. We use Firebase Authentication for secure login and Firestore security rules to restrict data access. Only you can modify your own profile data. Conversations are only accessible by participants.

5. Your Rights and Choices

  • Edit your profile: You can update or remove any profile information at any time through the Edit Profile page.
  • Delete your account: You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from the Profile page. This removes your profile, messages, crew connections, and projects.
  • Stealth Mode: Pro subscribers can enable Stealth Mode to hide their profile from Discover while retaining all other functionality.
  • Block users: You can block any user, preventing them from seeing your profile or contacting you.
  • Location: You can deny location permissions. The app will still function, but location-based features will be limited.
  • Notifications: You can disable push notifications through your device settings at any time.
  • Marketing emails: You can opt out of marketing communications at any time through the app settings on your Profile page, or by using the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.

6. Children's Privacy

Acru is not intended for users under 17 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 17, we will delete it promptly.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your profile data, messages, and associated content. Some data may be retained in backups for up to 30 days after deletion. Anonymized usage data may be retained for analytics purposes.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the app or via email. Your continued use of Acru after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
